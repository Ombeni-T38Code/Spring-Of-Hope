MISSION & VISION

Our Mission & Vision

Supporting refugees and host communities through locally led solutions that promote dignity, resilience, self-reliance, and sustainable development.

At Springs of Hope, our mission and vision guide every initiative we undertake, ensuring our impact is rooted in the voices of those we serve.

Our Mission

To empower refugees and host communities through education, sustainable livelihoods, and mental health support, fostering an environment where every individual has the agency to rebuild their lives.
