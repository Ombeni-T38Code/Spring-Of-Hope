Identity

A Community-Led Organization Creating Lasting Opportunities

Springs of Hope is a formally registered Refugee-Led Organization (RLO) and Community Based Organization (CBO) operating at the heart of the Kakuma Refugee Camp and Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement in Turkana West, Kenya.

We are not just service providers; we are members of the community we serve. Our leadership is composed entirely of individuals who have lived experience of displacement, ensuring our programs are culturally relevant, dignifying, and effective.

By leveraging local talent and fostering inclusive partnerships, we aim to transform displacement into an opportunity for growth, self-reliance, and dignity for all community members.

Narrative

Every Journey Begins with Hope

Our journey began with a simple observation: the most sustainable solutions come from within. Born from the collective aspiration of displaced people in Turkana West, Springs of Hope was founded to bridge the gap between aid and agency.

Starting as a small volunteer group, we focused on immediate peer support. Over time, we realized that we could achieve much more by formalizing our efforts and creating structured programs that address the root causes of vulnerability.

Today, we stand as a beacon of resilience, proving that with the right support and local leadership, refugee communities can drive their own development and contribute meaningfully to their host regions.

Our Reach

Where We Work

Kakuma Refugee Camp

Established in 1992, Kakuma hosts over 200,000 refugees. We work across all four camp sectors to provide vital support services, community hubs, and vocational training centers.

location_on Kakuma, Turkana West

Kalobeyei Settlement

A unique integrated settlement model focusing on self-reliance. We implement programs that foster economic collaboration between refugees and the host community.

Turkana Host Communities

Ensuring local Turkana people benefit from our shared resource programs, fostering peaceful coexistence and mutual prosperity through agricultural and education initiatives.

Our Values

Why Choose Springs of Hope

Our values define our approach and ensure our impact is both deep and lasting.

Community-Led

Decisions are made by those who live the reality of displacement every day, ensuring solutions are authentic and effective.

Inclusive

We embrace diversity and ensure all marginalized voices, including women and PWDs, are heard and served equally.

Transparent

Commitment to full accountability with our partners, donors, and the community we serve at every stage.

Sustainable

Focusing on long-term self-reliance and environmental stewardship rather than short-term relief cycles.
